Stage 01 of 05
Invoice Receipt & Capture
"Some of these things are as clean and as easy as you could possibly imagine — and some of them are written with a crayon on the back of a napkin. Whatever range you could imagine is what we see." — Jeff Wilson, ArcBest · Discovery Session
Pain points from the workshop
Missing invoice notification — no alert when expected invoices don't arrive
OCR static at ~70% (Non-EDI) — built Feb 2025, not learning. Requires manual review queue and data entry fallback.
Mailbox queries overload — 3 emails/day per person just managing AP inbox noise (statements, promos, non-invoices)
55k EDI invoices/month — EDI coding comes from supplier, not always reliable or complete
13k PDF invoices/month via email — manual coding: Tribal knowledge, printed on invoice, manual entry
1,700 paper invoices/month — scanned at HQ and service centers, emailed into system
OCR validation loop into ERP: King Out → STP process adds cycle time
How basware solves it
SmartPDFAI Capture
AI capture that learns from every correction — no static ceiling. Handles all formats: EDI, PDF, email, scan, paper. Automatically extracts header and line-item data. Missing invoice alerts built in.
Guardian
Intelligent inbox filtering — automatically removes statements, promos, and non-invoice documents before they reach your AP team. Eliminates the 3-emails/day mailbox management burden.
Basware Network
Structured supplier portal for invoice submission — removes paper and email invoices at source. Gives vendors real-time status visibility, cutting payment inquiries just like Triumph Pay.
Stage 02 of 05
Validation & Matching
"How do we know that what we're being charged is in line with the contract? There's no great way to audit that unless you're manually digging through thousands of invoices. That's Goal #3 — reduce spend leakage." — Jeff Wilson, ArcBest · Discovery Session
Pain points from the workshop
No systematic matching — no PO environment. Matching is procedural, not system-enforced.
No contract compliance — Cintas, Waste Management billed at unknown rates vs. contracts. Spend leakage undetected.
GL coding: manual — coded by supplier (EDI), tribal knowledge, manual entry, or printed on invoice. No AI assistance.
Valid GL code? — team must verify coding is correct. Second-touch validation required for SOD compliance.
No auto-approval tolerance rules for recurring predictable spend (utilities, national accounts).
Recovery audit only happens every 2 years — overbilling goes undetected between cycles.
How basware solves it
Smart Coding
AI GL code suggestions trained on ArcBest's own historical coding patterns — eliminates tribal knowledge dependency, manual entry, and the double-touch coding validation step.
AP Assurance
Continuous contract compliance — validates every Cintas, Waste Management, and national account invoice against contracted rates automatically. No more 2-year recovery audit gap. Spend leakage caught at invoice.
AP Protect
Exact and fuzzy duplicate detection, vendor validation, and fraud signal identification across the full invoice population before approval routing.
Stage 03 of 05
Approval Workflow
"DOA Workday — escalation approver not working. Based on coding. We have custom routing. There's 150 invoices in your queue — go see how quick you can kick those out. I'm not confident we have comprehensive review." — Jeff Wilson, ArcBest · Discovery Session
Pain points from the workshop
DOA (Delegation of Authority) in Workday — escalation approver is broken / not functioning correctly
Routing based on coding — GL code determines route, creating dependency on accurate coding at Stage 2
~150 invoices per approver queue — rubber-stamping at scale. No genuine review of charges or fraud signals.
Escalation approval broken — invoices stuck when primary approver unavailable, no working fallback
Custom routing required for PO, vendor, and GL-based flows — fragile, hard to maintain
No mobile or email-based one-click approval — approvers must log into the system manually
How basware solves it
Smart Workflow
Replaces the fragile Workday-based routing. Fully configurable rules: route by GL code, cost center, vendor, PO, amount, company code, or custom criteria. Workday DOA hierarchy integrated. Escalation and delegation automation built in — no more broken escalation paths.
AI Business Agent
Auto-approves recurring spend within tolerance — utilities, national accounts, predictable invoices. Queue drops from 150 → 20–30 real exceptions. One-click email approval for the remainder. Approvers only see what genuinely needs their eyes.
AP Automation
Mobile approvals, working delegation and escalation chains, full audit trail per decision. Business approvers freed for high-value work — Goal #1 achieved.
Stage 04 of 05
Reporting & Analytics
"Spend visibility, liabilities, discounts, benchmarking — spend should be contextual. We do a recovery audit every two years. It shouldn't take that long to find out we've been overbilled." — Jeff Wilson, ArcBest · Discovery Session
Pain points from the workshop
No spend visibility — can't see liabilities, accruals, or what's in-flight at any moment
No discounts visibility — early pay discount opportunities missed; no system to surface or capture them
Zero native AP reporting — no dashboards, no KPIs built in. Everything pulled and assembled manually.
Benchmarking — spend should be contextual but no benchmarking capability exists today
Recovery audit only every 2 years — overbilling by vendors goes undetected between cycles
Cycle time tracked manually. No touchless rate, exception rate, or cost per invoice metric.
How basware solves it
AI Data Agent
Natural language analytics — ask "what's our liability exposure this month?" or "which vendors have outstanding invoices over 30 days?" Get instant answers. No SQL, no manual pulls, no data house report needed.
AI Business Agent
Proactive spend visibility — surfaces early pay discount opportunities, flags liabilities, benchmarks vendor spend against contract and history. Replaces the 2-year recovery audit with continuous monitoring.
AP Assurance
Pre-built KPI dashboards: cycle time, touchless rate, exception rate, cost per invoice, spend by GL and cost center. Full audit log. BI tool export. Addresses Goals #2 and #3 directly.
Stage 05 of 05
ERP Integration
"Theoretically it would be one piece in the stack — connecting to AP, gabsTEC, AFE, MMS, GL, Workday. The routing system is too fragile. We need something we can trust." — Jeff Wilson, ArcBest · Discovery Session
Pain points from the workshop
Homegrown ERP ("AP system") — one instance, company-code siloed. Has API but no standard connector. gabsTEC middleware connects to AFE, MMS, GL, and Workday.
No middleware preferred for AP — wants clean point-to-point integration managed by ArcBest Technologies
Multiple company codes with separate GL and cost center master data (owned by Accounting team)
Phased rollout required — no rip-and-replace. Target cycle time: 1 day end-to-end.
How basware solves it
AP Automation
API-first architecture connects directly to ArcBest's homegrown AP system — no middleware layer added. 250+ ERP integrations track record. ArcBest Technologies team engaged as integration counterpart.
AP Assurance
Validated ERP posting with full audit trail. GL, cost center, AFE, and MMS master data sync from source systems. Multi-entity and multi-company-code support out of the box.
Basware Network
Phased rollout methodology. Test + production environments. 90-day hypercare post go-live. Dedicated support for ArcBest Technologies team through integration build.
